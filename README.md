# Soul-Bound

A 3D horror-investigation game built in Unity, developed as a team project 
for a game development course. Players take on the role of a journalist 
investigating the mysterious deaths of the Johnson family, trapped inside 
a manor sealed by a curse that binds both the family's spirits and anyone 
who enters.

Inspired by *Resident Evil*, *Outlast*, and *Amnesia: The Dark Descent*, 
the game blends survival-horror tension with an optional narrative layer: 
players complete the game by piecing together the full story of the family's 
murder by collecting storytelling items scattered across its two floors.

## Gameplay

- **Objective**: Escape the manor by finding items; 
  collecting storytelling items to uncover the full narrative.
- **Mechanics**: Move, sprint, crouch, hide, pick up and manage items via 
  an inventory system, interact with pursuing enemies.
- **Resources**: Regenerating health (via hiding), limited stamina, a 
  battery-powered flashlight, and designated hiding spots.
- **Levels**: Basement, first floor, and second floor, each with distinct 
  items, threats, and paths toward escape.
